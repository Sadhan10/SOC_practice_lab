How to complete Operation Black Falcon

Challenge Flow

Open an investigation, answer the analyst prompts correctly, unlock the investigation flag, then submit it from the flag console.

Rules

All investigations are available immediately. Flags must be submitted only after the findings are validated. Progress is tracked per username.

Scope

This is a local OVA-based training environment. Stay inside the simulated Black Falcon application and evidence data.

Flag Format

32-character case token
